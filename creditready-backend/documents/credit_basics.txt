Credit Basics

A credit score is a numerical indicator that lenders may use to assess a person's creditworthiness. The scoring scale and criteria depend on the credit bureau and scoring model.

Payment history is an important factor in many credit scoring models. Paying bills and loan instalments on time can help maintain a positive credit history. Missed payments may negatively affect a credit score.

Credit utilisation describes the amount of revolving credit being used compared with the available credit limit. High utilisation may negatively affect some credit scores.

A loan is money borrowed from a lender that must be repaid according to the loan agreement. Depending on the agreement, interest and fees may apply.

EMI stands for Equated Monthly Instalment. It is a scheduled payment made by a borrower toward a loan. An EMI commonly includes principal and interest.

Before taking a loan, borrowers should compare interest rates, processing fees, repayment periods, and other applicable charges. They should also consider whether the monthly repayments fit their budget.

Credit scores and lending criteria vary by country, credit bureau, lender, and scoring model. Borrowers should consult their credit bureau or lender for details relevant to their circumstances.
